Your trust, kept

Your life stays yours.

We built Stevens the way we'd want it for our own homes. Trust isn't a setting here — it's the foundation.

You decide what's shared

Stevens asks before it shares anything — and it remembers your “no.” The personal stays personal.

It listens only when you tap

No always-on microphone, ever. Stevens speaks when you tap and talk — and rests the rest of the time.

It never acts as you

Stevens works for you, never as you. It won't send from your partner's account or move money on its own.

Your own private vault

What Stevens knows lives in a vault that's yours alone — encrypted, and never shared across households.

See it. Change it. Revoke it.

Every permission is visible and reversible in a tap. You're always in control of what Stevens can reach.

We equip you. You decide.

A tool is leverage. Stevens extends what you can do; the choices remain entirely yours.
