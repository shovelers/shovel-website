02. Philosophy · What a tool is
Essay · 4 min read

A tool is leverage.

A spear, a shovel, a language, a protocol, an agent. The tool extends what a person can do — and the person decides what to do with it.

We don't pretend the tool decides. We equip the human. The human decides.

"Tools are neutral. Agency belongs to the person holding them."

¹ This line runs under everything we build. It is also why we name our products after their purpose, not their cleverness.

03. Stevens · Voice-first personal agent
Product · in development

Meet
Stevens.

The brain of the family. Voice-first. Always listening for what matters, never for what doesn't.

No typing. No tapping through menus. You speak, Stevens responds — in voice, with the small visual prompts you need to confirm an action.

In between calls, Stevens hibernates. Watching the household quietly. Surfacing what's overdue, what's coming, what you'd want to know.
